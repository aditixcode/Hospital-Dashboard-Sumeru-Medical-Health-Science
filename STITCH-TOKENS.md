## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the clinical authority, intellectual rigor, and compassionate composure of a premier tier-one academic medical center. It rejects transient tech tropes—such as floating multi-color neon blobs, decorative glassmorphism, aggressive gradient buttons, and playful micro-animations—in favor of structural dignity, high visual legibility, and clinical precision. 

The aesthetic is grounded in contemporary editorial minimalism paired with institutional clarity. White and clean slate planes are organized through precise structural hairlines, generous whitespace, authoritative classical typography for scholarly leadership, and ultra-crisp modern sans-serifs for complex clinical data presentation. The target audience encompasses patients seeking reassuring, friction-free care, as well as world-class clinicians, researchers, and fellows who require immediate, unambiguous data density. The emotional tone is deliberate, reassuring, clear, and quiet—reducing cognitive load during moments of high physiological and psychological stress.

## Colors

The color palette is deliberately fixed to an anti-glare, high-contrast light model engineered for critical clinical readability and ambient hospital conditions.

- **Primary (`#0B1B3D`, Obsidian Navy):** Anchors institutional identity, major navigational headers, high-authority brand elements, and primary typographic weight.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A`, Clinical Royal Blue):** Used for sub-navigation, authoritative section markers, department flags, and academic badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`, Surgical Cyan):** Reserved for primary interactive affordances, key calls to action, focus indicators, active tabs, and primary interactive links.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`, Clean Slate Canvas):** Establishes an expansive, low-fatigue workspace background across all desktop and mobile surfaces.

### Semantic & Clinical Guardrails
Clinical statuses use a strict three-tier token contract (Text, Background, Border) to ensure accessibility standards and avoid relying on color alone:
- **Critical / Emergency (`#991B1B` / `#FEF2F2` / `#FCA5A5`):** Reserved strictly for urgent medical flags (e.g., SpO2 < 90%, severe anaphylactic allergies, crash cart triggers, ICU alerts).
- **Warning / Elevated (`#92400E` / `#FFFBEB` / `#FCD34D`):** Borderline lab results, unverified insurance, pending physician sign-offs.
- **Normal / Stable (`#166534` / `#F0FDF4` / `#86EFAC`):** Cleared clinical metrics, normal diagnostic results, open appointment windows.
- **Emergency Action (`#BE123C`):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate patient hotlines, code blue workflows, and triage escalations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces a disciplined division of labor between editorial authority and data-dense clinical clarity:

1. **Editorial & Scholarly Display (`Newsreader`):** Applied exclusively to major landing headers, clinical institute titles, and section-level anchors. It conveys academic research prestige, humanistic medicine, and institutional legacy.
2. **Interface & Narrative Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and welcoming sans-serif engineered for effortless scanning across long clinical notes, patient summaries, triage forms, and administrative layouts.
3. **Data Density & Tabular Figures (`Inter`):** Applied to badges, uppercase clinical tags, metadata chips, and vital metrics. Any numeric data subject to real-time fluctuation (heart rates, blood pressures, lab metrics) must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` to eliminate jitter and visual misalignment.

### Editorial Measure
Body paragraphs must never exceed a maximum measure of 72 characters (`~680px`), ensuring low ocular fatigue for clinicians reading continuous EHR notes and patients reviewing procedural guidelines.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8-point grid** with a disciplined **4-point half-step** for micro-components, badges, and high-density tabular environments.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Viewport Strategies
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** Single-column stacked workflow. Interactive components expand to 100% container width with strict 44px minimum vertical tap targets. A sticky bottom utility bar provides immediate access to emergency triage and phone lines.
- **Tablet (`640px – 1023px`):** 8-column layout. Introduces split master-detail drawer navigation for patient queues and scheduling grids.
- **Desktop (`1024px – 1439px`):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` maximum content width for editorial and general patient portal pages.
- **Clinical Workstation (`1440px+`):** Full-bleed, 4-pane continuous EHR workspace:
  1. *Global Navigation Strip* (64px icon rail)
  2. *Patient Census & Registry* (340px fixed master drawer)
  3. *Primary Chart & Medical Record* (Fluid middle column)
  4. *Diagnostic Context & Vitals Telemetry* (380px fixed side tray)

All layout containers align strictly to internal card paddings: `space-md` (16px) for standard records and `space-lg` (24px) for major workspace frames and specialty clinical hubs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via flat, layered white containers stacked over the `#F8FAFC` slate canvas, bounded by crisp `#E2E8F0` structural hairlines. 

### Elevation Rules
- **No Frosted Glass:** Backdrop filters, blurred layers, and ambient colored neomorphism are strictly prohibited to prevent legibility degradation under varying clinic ambient lighting.
- **Subtle Layering:** Surface elevation is established primarily through hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), with low-opacity navy drop shadows used sparingly to denote interactive depth:
  - **Resting Surface:** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.05)` with `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`
  - **Interactive Hover:** `transform: translateY(-2px); box-shadow: 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.08); border-color: #CBD5E1`
  - **Modal / Flyout Overlays:** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Focus Rings:** Accessibility dual-ring system consisting of a crisp `2px #FFFFFF` inner offset ring followed by a `4px #0284C7` outer halo.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a restrained **Soft (`1`)** shape language that balances clinical modernism with professional stability.

- **Status Badges, Chips, Pills:** `4px` (`rounded-sm`). Compact, crisp, and space-efficient for high-density tabular environments.
- **Form Controls & Buttons:** `6px` (`rounded-md`). Provides tactile comfort without softening the institutional posture.
- **Cards, Clinical Data Containers, Tables:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`). Ensures clean boundary definitions and natural structural containment.
- **Modals, Sheets, Clinical Diagnostic Trays:** `12px` (`rounded-xl`). Applied only to root-level overlays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0284C7` (Surgical Cyan) background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#0369A1` with an immediate `150ms ease-in-out` transition. Radius is `6px`. Font: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px, weight `600`. Padding: `10px 20px`.
- **Institutional Primary / Academic:** Solid `#0B1B3D` (Obsidian Navy) background with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for main site header CTAs and formal institutional actions.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover background shifts to `#F8FAFC` with border darkening to `#0B1B3D`.
- **Emergency Button:** Solid `#BE123C` fill, `#FFFFFF` bold text, with immediate phone/alert icon. Hover shifts to `#9F1239`.

### Chips & Clinical Status Badges
- Displayed with an uppercase tracking rule (`font-size: 11px`, `letter-spacing: 0.05em`, `font-weight: 500`).
- Padding: `2px 8px`, border radius: `4px`.
- Must match the triad color definitions (e.g., Stable: `#F0FDF4` bg, `#86EFAC` border, `#166534` text). An explicit icon (e.g., checkmark, warning triangle, or pulse dot) must accompany the label to ensure accessibility.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Height `42px` (desktop), `48px` (mobile). Surface: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Radius: `6px`. Text: `#0F172A` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px).
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `2px solid #0284C7` with a `3px` diffuse halo of `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
- **Error State:** Border shifts to `2px solid #BE123C` with text feedback rendered in `#991B1B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px × 18px` with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Active checked state fills with `#0284C7` with a crisp white tick.

### Cards & Workstation Panels
- Structural `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by `1px solid #E2E8F0` with subtle elevation. No heavy dropped drop-shadows or gradients.
- Header: Internal divider line separating card title (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 18px, weight `600`) from card content.
- Interactive cards elevate `-2px` on hover with border transitioning to `#CBD5E1`.

### Data Tables & Clinical Registry
- Header rows: `#F1F5F9` background, `#64748B` uppercase micro-labels (`11px`), `1px solid #E2E8F0` bottom border.
- Data rows: `#FFFFFF` background with alternating row capability for multi-column triage sheets. Cell padding: `12px 16px`. Numbers rendered with tabular lining figures.

### Global Patient Banner (EHR Context)
- Height: Fixed `52px` positioned directly below the application navigation bar.
- Background: `#0B1B3D` with `#FFFFFF` high-contrast patient name (`16px`, semi-bold), MRN (`13px`, `#93C5FD`), age/gender, and immediate right-aligned red allergy alert badges.
